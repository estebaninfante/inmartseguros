## Platform

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS — one-page landing, `index.html` + `assets/`, no build step. User left the stack open ("usa la tecnología que mejor te sirva"); chosen for zero-dependency deploy anywhere and fastest handoff.

## Users

Executives, contractors, and procurement teams at Colombian construction and health companies that bid on tenders (licitaciones) and need bonding policies (pólizas de cumplimiento) plus corporate insurance advisory. They scan for credibility in seconds: trajectory, technical command of tender requirements, direct access to a real advisor.

## Product Purpose

One-page marketing site for INMART ASESORES DE SEGUROS LTDA, Colombian corporate insurance advisory and brokerage. Makes the visitor understand in seconds that INMART protects high-stakes projects with 15+ years of experience, and drives direct contact (WhatsApp/email). Success: visitor contacts INMART.

## Positioning

Not an insurer, not a generic agency: a specialized advisor with technical command of licitaciones and pólizas de cumplimiento. Primary target sector: construction; also health; able to advise any industry with a project or contract to protect. Trajectory: independent activity since 2007, legally consolidated 2015.

## Operating Context

Colombian insurance intermediation. Tenders follow oferta → adjudicación → ejecución, each step requiring surety policies (seriedad de oferta, cumplimiento). Contact is direct and personal, with the gerente general.
